About Us TCU Smart Clinic

The TCU Smart Clinic Appointment and Queue System is a web-based platform designed to make campus clinic services more convenient and organized.

The system is intended for students of Taguig City University. It allows authorized users to request clinic appointments and check their appointment status.

Through this system, we aim to improve appointment management, help organize clinic queues, and make clinic information easier to access.

Our Purpose

To provide TCU Students with a simpler way to request clinic appointments and monitor queue information and access clinic service.
