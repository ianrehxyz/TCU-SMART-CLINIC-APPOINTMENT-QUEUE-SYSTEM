FOUR SIMPLE STEPS

How It Works

Follow these steps to request and monitor a clinic appointment.

01

Create an Account

Register using the information required by the TCU Smart Clinic system.

02

Log In

Access your account using your registered login credentials.

03

Request an Appointment

Complete the appointment form, provide the required details, and submit your request.

04

Check Your Status

Monitor your request and check your queue number when it is assigned.

Remember: Submitting an appointment request does not automatically mean it is approved. Wait for the clinic's decision and check your status.
