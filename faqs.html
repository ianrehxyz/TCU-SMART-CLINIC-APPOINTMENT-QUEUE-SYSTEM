Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the TCU Smart Clinic system.

Who can use the TCU Smart Clinic system?

The system is intended for TCU students subject to the university's account access requirements.

How do I request a clinic appointment?

Sign up or log in, open the appointment form, enter the required information, and submit your request.

Is my appointment automatically approved?

No. Your request must be reviewed. Check your appointment status to see the clinic's decision.

How can I get my queue number?

Your queue number should be available after your appointment has been approved and the clinic has assigned a number.

Can I monitor my appointment status?

Yes, if the status feature is connected to the database, you can view the latest status of your request through your account.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the password recovery feature on the login page if it has been configured.

Does the system replace the clinic's healthcare services?

No. It helps manage appointments and queue information. Medical assessment and treatment are still provided by authorized clinic personnel.
